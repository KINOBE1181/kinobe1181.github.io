About Me

I am a graduate student pursuing my Master's degree in Computer Science at Rowan University. My interests include artificial intelligence, cybersecurity, database systems, and distributed computing.

Through my academic coursework and independent learning, I have gained experience working with AI technologies, database transactions, and parallel programming. I am especially interested in understanding how complex computer systems operate, how problems arise within them, and how those systems can be made more reliable and secure.

My goal is to continue developing my technical skills while preparing for opportunities in software engineering, AI engineering, cybersecurity, and systems-related roles.
